## Hero

# Repair updates without the daily phone calls.

ClaimTrack helps collision shops keep customers updated with repair status, parts-delay notes, insurance steps, photo proof, pickup checklists, and review follow-ups.

**CTA:** Open ClaimTrack

## What It Replaces

- Repeated status calls
- Missed customer updates
- Parts-delay notes scattered across desks
- Insurance supplement follow-up in text threads
- Photo updates sent one by one
- Pickup steps handled from memory
- Review requests forgotten after delivery

## Core Modules

### Overview
Metrics for active repairs, insurance holds, parts delays, and vehicles ready for pickup.

### Repairs
Repair order list with customer, vehicle, RO number, claim number, status, ETA, delay reason, and next update.

### Parts
Part name, status, ETA, delay reason, and linked repair record.

### Insurance
Claim notes, supplement status, adjuster contact, and approval status.

### Photos
Uploaded repair photos labeled by repair stage: check-in damage, tear-down, parts, body work, paint, and finished vehicle.

### Pickup
Checklist for final wash, quality check, customer notice, deductible, paperwork, keys, and review request.

### Updates
Send text or email drafts for repair complete, review request, and 30-day check-in.
